Step 1 of 4 · Value Ranking
What matters most to you?
A few things can outweigh many. That is the weight of your values.
Other assessments treat all areas of life as equally important. You don't.
Values matter and we don't make that mistake. A student finding their path, a devoted parent, an ambitious entrepreneur, an activist working for change, each would rank these eleven domains completely differently. And every one of them is right.

Before we measure anything, we need to know what matters most to you. Read through the domains on the next screen, then sort and prioritize them in a way that aligns with living your truth, most important at the top, least important at the bottom. We included a Domain Guide on the right, where you can see what each life domain entails.

There are no right answers here. Only honest ones.
Step 2 of 4 · Satisfaction & Direction
How are you doing, honestly?
The life you deserve comes from knowing where you are now, and where you're heading.
Life is fluid and things change, but this is your moment to check in without comparing or performing. We have two questions for you here.

1) Listen to your inner voice. How satisfied do you genuinely feel in each area right now? Give each domain a number from 1 to 10.

2) Let's zoom out a little. Compared to the past 3 months, is each area getting better, staying the same, or getting worse?

Remember, a low satisfaction number is not a failure. It is simply a place that wants your love and attention.
Step 3 of 4 · Readiness
Where will you put your fire this season?
Four ways to say yes. One way to say not yet.
We all have limited resources: time, energy, attention, money. This is not about giving up on any area of life. It is about being strategically honest about where you can meaningfully invest right now.

Trying to work on everything leads to progress on nothing. The areas you set aside this season are not abandoned. They are waiting, and we will hold them for you in your Next Season Watchlist.
